The short version

This site is a static page. It runs no analytics, sets no tracking cookies, and has no database, login or contact form. Nothing you do while reading it is recorded here.

What this site loads

Two third parties are contacted when the page opens, and each one sees your IP address and browser details as part of any normal web request:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) — serves the typefaces.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) — serves the GSAP animation library.

The site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs.

When you buy

Checkout happens on Gumroad, not here. Gumroad collects your email address, payment details and whatever tax information the sale requires, and acts as the merchant of record. Payment details never pass through this site and are never seen by the author.

After a purchase, the author receives your email address and order details through Gumroad, and uses them only to deliver the book and answer questions about your order.

Your rights

For purchase data, exercise your rights (access, correction, deletion, marketing opt-out) through Gumroad, which holds that data. For anything else, email [YOUR EMAIL].
